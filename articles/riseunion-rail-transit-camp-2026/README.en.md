# RiseUnion Wins Compute Scheduling Platform Contract with Major Rail Transit Equipment Manufacturer

RiseUnion · April 03, 2026 at 08:00 (UTC+08:00)

RiseUnion has won the compute scheduling platform procurement contract with a major domestic rail transit equipment manufacturer. Powered by Rise CAMP, the deployment will unify Nvidia, Ascend, Hygon, and Cambricon accelerators on a self-controlled compute foundation that supports intelligent train control and connected-vehicle innovation.

RiseUnion recently **won the compute scheduling platform procurement contract with a major domestic rail transit equipment manufacturer**, leveraging deep core technology and proven delivery capability to deliver a self-controlled intelligent-compute foundation that powers intelligent train control and connected-vehicle innovation.

## Product Highlights and Value

[Rise CAMP](https://www.theriseunion.com/en/product/camp.html) is RiseUnion's AI compute scheduling platform. Through intelligent resource scheduling, workload optimization, automation, and cross-platform support, it helps enterprises and AI teams manage large-scale compute efficiently — especially for AI development and training workloads.

- **Boundary-free management**: Rise CAMP unifies Nvidia, Ascend, Hygon, Cambricon, and other heterogeneous accelerators, removing hardware silos and reducing supply-chain risk.
